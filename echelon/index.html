Why Echelon exists

Built for what comes after success.

You've built something most people will never have the experience of building. You want to steward it well and create massive impact for the kingdom. What's in the way is that the problems at this level are ones nobody around you can help with, so you carry the big decisions alone, success keeps pulling at who you are, and home gets what's left.

Echelon is the Man on a Mission Advisory Board. You work one-on-one with Matt, and once a month you sit at the Boardroom Call with Christian business owners doing $50,000 or more a month. You bring the decisions you can't bring anywhere else: the acquisition, the capital, the marriage, the temptation. The Word has the final say.

Joining is simple. You apply, you have a conversation with Matt, and you take your seat. If you ignore it, the cost is the one nobody puts on a business plan: you become a stranger to your kids and a roommate to your wife. If you join, you get a table of men who will tell you the truth and hold you to what matters most.

One 90-minute Boardroom Call a month with the board, your one-on-one time with Matt, and you're welcome on every other call in Man on a Mission.
