Your guide

I almost lost everything. It all stemmed from making my work an idol.

If you're the hardest worker anyone knows and the people at home still get what's left of you, I understand. I lived it.

I built my identity on a business, lost over $1.2 million when the lockdowns shut my gym down five times, burnt out, and found steady only when I rebuilt the foundation in Christ. For ten years I've helped hundreds of Christian entrepreneurs make the same shift.

Read the whole story

Between 2020 and 2023 the lockdowns shut us down five times. Seven months paying rent on a 10,000 square foot building I couldn't use. We came dangerously close to bankruptcy. The money wasn't the hardest part. I'd spent thirteen years building my identity on being a successful entrepreneur. My work had quietly become my idol. When the business was threatened, my identity felt threatened.

I'd been going hard for years before that, and I burnt out. It showed up as an autoimmune condition. The only thing that gave me any relief was getting into cold water before 5am, so I kept doing it. A year or two in I noticed something that had nothing to do with my immune system. I was steadier. I'd make a decision and stay with it, in the business, at home, in the way I talked to my wife on a hard day.

Rebuilding my identity in Christ changed everything. Now I work from approval instead of for approval. Consistent across all five areas of life, the same at home as at work. That's what I build with every man I coach.
